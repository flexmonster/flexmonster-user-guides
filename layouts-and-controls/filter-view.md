# Filter view

A filter view is used to configure filtering and sorting for a specific field.

This guide describes how to open the filter view and what you can do in it.

## Open the filter view

On the grid, select the field’s name. As a result, the filter view will appear as a dialog:

![The filter view](../assets/images/data-shaping/simple_filter.png)

The filter view is available in both the pivot and the flat table.

Note that your application can also display the filter view as a panel next to the grid. In this case, the filter view is always visible and is configured for one specific field.

## What you can configure

In the filter view, you can:

- Filter the field by its members—select the members you want to see on the grid using the checkboxes or the search box.
- Filter the field by a condition—select **Add condition** to show only the members that meet the condition you specify.
- Sort the field members—use the **AZ** and **ZA** toggle buttons next to **Sort**.
- Override the field caption—edit the caption at the top of the filter view.

The member filter and the conditional filter can both be applied to the same field.

## Apply or discard your changes

Your changes aren't reflected on the grid until you select **Apply** in the filter view.

To close the filter view without applying the changes, select **Cancel**.
